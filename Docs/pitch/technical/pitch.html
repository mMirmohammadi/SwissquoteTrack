Q&A / CRYPTOGRAPHY AND ADDRESS RULES

What changes — and what stays the same?

CRYPTOGRAPHY USED

Owner and card

Owner: BIP-39, 256-bit entropy, 24 words.
Card: BIP-39, 128-bit entropy, 12 words.

EVM signing

BIP-32 path m/44'/60'/0'/0/0.
secp256k1 owner and guardian signatures.

Encryption at rest

AES-256-GCM; random salt and IV.
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256, 600,000 rounds.

ADDRESS RULES

Existing EVM Safe

Owner rotates; deployed Safe address stays.
Other EVM chains require their own deployment.

Plain key-based Solana / Bitcoin

New key means new derived addresses.
No multi-chain recovery shipped here.

Possible Solana extension

A program-controlled account could keep a stable address while rotating authority. Requires design and audit.

BIP-39 encodes entropy as words; it is not encryption. The backup preserves access only while its owner remains current.

Q&A / LIMITS AND EMERGENCY EXIT

What the safety net does not promise.

Service controls

Screening and passkeys govern the sponsor. Another funded submitter can relay a valid owner signature.

Bank unavailable

Current Emergency Kit + its code recover the owner seed locally. An independent gas payer and working chain are still needed.

Secrets compromised

Owner leak: immediate spending risk.
Card leak: replace that guardian too.
Lost funds: no rollback.

A kit-based transfer was a separate scripted test, not a full live outage drill. Physical-device passkeys, production identity assurance, helper custody and legal classification need further work.
