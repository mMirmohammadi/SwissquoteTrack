01 · THE CLIENT · Own keys should not mean leaving Swissquote.

Meet Claire, an illustrative client. Today the brief says clients withdraw to an external wallet for self-custody. Our aim is to keep the Swissquote relationship without giving the bank unilateral control. This is a hackathon prototype, not an approved Swissquote product.

02 · THE PRODUCT · Three objects. Three clear jobs.

The phone signs payments. The Recovery Card authorizes a replacement with one helper. The Emergency Kit is an encrypted copy of the key, opened with a separate code. No creation clip is packaged: explain creation here rather than pretend a recording is available. The bank holds a helper key, not the owner payment key.

These are existing recorded browser runs through the app, not transactions being sent by this deck. Both receipts are public. Compound is a third-party test deployment, not an official market. 06 · LEGAL POSITION · Client control is the argument — not regulatory approval.

Our legal position rests on the bank lacking unilateral owner-key control. It is not a settled legal conclusion. Client card plus Swissquote recovery and the role of a fee-paying service are hard questions for counsel. A wallet signature proves control; it does not replace KYC or solve every Travel Rule obligation. The supplied brief is our challenge source. The plans refer to FINMA 02/2019, 08/2023 section 2.2 and the 2018 DLT report, but this lane has not independently revalidated those legal citations.

07 · BUSINESS · Clients keep their keys. Swissquote keeps the relationship.

The business case is retention: self-custody can remain one step from the client’s bank account and trading relationship. We use a formula, not the unconfirmed CHF 640,000 scenario. The pilot should measure retained clients, incremental trading, revenue rate, recovery demand and fee costs. The CHF 4.90 subscription is an untested pricing hypothesis, not measured willingness to pay.

Passkey approval is a service rule, not a contract restriction. The wait is a cancellation window, not a guarantee that a user will see a notice. We have no external audit of our composition.
