## Gasless: who pays and what proves it

The owner signs a Safe transaction; Swissquote's funded relayer submits it and pays native gas. This is **Safe relay sponsorship**, not an ERC-4337 paymaster/bundler. A wallet can send USDC with zero ETH. Prove this with service-sent RELAYER receipts and wallet ETH before/after. The [Sepolia story](<claims.md>) proves externally paid scripted transactions with wallet ETH 0, including mined payments and recovery: TREASURY submits those sends and FUNDER pays the 3-USDC RPC-only kit sweep. It does not prove Swissquote-service RELAYER use. The large payment's `requiresPasskey:true` is classification, not WebAuthn execution.

Abuse controls target approved wallet deployment, pre-send simulation, strict call eligibility, per-account/global request limits, gas/fee caps, zero refund fields and exact-transaction-hash passkey step-up for large/admin calls. Screening/budget outages must not block protective cancellation through the ordinary-spend policy; dedicated caps, positive wallet hits and operational outages still limit it. Owner self-funded sends bypass sponsor policy. [Costs](<business-case.md>) distinguish simulated gas, actual test-ETH fees and mainnet extrapolations.
