# Independent registration review — source snapshot

## Result

The inspected service source fences verified-wallet persistence after the registration deadline and pins rules plus ownership proof to the selected block. The remaining observed issues are in UI activity fencing and the scope of RPC pacing. Fix those before treating the bounded correction as complete.

This is an **as-of-read review of work in flight**, not a security certification or a claim about the final integrated revision. Source files were read sequentially and may evolve. No commands, tests, runtime, network, keys, or Git were used. Only this new report was written. Existing test source was inspected, not executed; no test-pass claim is made.

Review basis: [registration freshness critic](<../plans/v3/registration-freshness-critic.md>). The full human-facing communication policy and track background were read. The user specified a source-only scope, so no Git baseline or tracker lookup was attempted.

## Spec findings

### Medium — registration can still sign after the device is locked

**Observed:** [registerCurrent](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L83-L91>) snapshots `signingGeneration`, but checks it only after the complete registration helper returns. [The helper](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/lifecycle.ts#L24-L26>) awaits a challenge, then derives the private key, signs, and posts registration without an activity check. [The visibility lock](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L173-L185>), [logout](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L428>), and [explicit lock](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L461>) invalidate the generation and clear React seed state, not the helper's captured seed.

**Concrete sequence:** start retry with an unlocked seed; hold the challenge response; lock or sign out; release the challenge. The helper can still sign and submit with the captured seed and token. The later generation check prevents a verified UI update, but cannot undo that signing or submission. This proof does not authorize a transfer; no funds-moving exploit is claimed.

**Recommendation:** provide an activity guard to the helper and check it after the challenge await, immediately before signing, after signing, and before POST. Capture the generation before fallible unlock/read stages as well; otherwise a lock during `unlocked()` or `readPending()` predates the current snapshot and is not detected. Critic expectations: explicit device unlock remains unchanged and stale identity cannot advance registration.

### Medium — replacement registration bypasses identity and stale-GET guards

**Observed:** [completeReplacement](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L334-L339>) calls `registerDeviceOwnership` directly and then unconditionally calls `setVerified(registration.verified)` and publishes a success notice. Unlike [registerCurrent](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L83-L91>), it checks neither signing generation nor wallet/session/owner identity and does not invalidate `ownershipGeneration`.

**Concrete consequences:** a replacement registration held until after logout can set verified state and a success notice in the signed-out/new identity UI. An ownership GET triggered by the promoted owner can also reject after this successful POST and overwrite success through [the GET effect](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L158-L170>). The service still performs ownership checks; this is an incorrect client publication/race, not a service authorization bypass.

**Recommendation:** apply equivalent guarded publication and stale-GET invalidation to this existing registration caller. Account for React's publication timing when checking the newly promoted owner; do not blindly assume state setters synchronously update the identity ref.

### Medium — registration pacing also changes simulation/gas/send-adjacent reads

**Observed:** [readHttp/publicClient construction](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L36-L43>) installs `pacedFetch` on the shared Sepolia public client. [pacedFetch](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L91-L104>) applies its endpoint queue and spacing even when no registration context exists. That client is used by [simulation](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L70-L72>), [gas estimation](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L133>), [fee reads](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L47-L50>), and owner/nonce reads.

**Consequence:** non-registration preflight reads are now queued and delayed, including behind registration history traffic on shared URLs. The wallet broadcast transport itself is separate; this is not a claim that broadcast was paced or sender reservations were released. It violates [the critic's explicit scope boundary](<../plans/v3/registration-freshness-critic.md#L43-L45>) not to pace or modify send/simulation/gas paths.

**Recommendation:** keep pacing scoped to registration read transports/context, without changing endpoint lists, retry architecture, or non-registration callers. Verify shared-role retry spacing within that scope.

## Standards

No separate documented-standard violation is asserted. This pass was bounded to safety mechanisms and the supplied critic, not a full style/smell audit. The three findings above are behavioral/spec findings, not speculative code-smell labels.

## Safety mechanisms observed in the service snapshot

- **Deadline/persistence:** [ALS context](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L27-L51>) uses one monotonic deadline, permanently closes and aborts expired reads, and races read stages only. [The handler](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/app.ts#L216-L223>) starts that context before `computeWallet`; [final persistence](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/app.ts#L250-L257>) follows the recheck and active assertions and uses a synchronous store transaction. No promise containing the wallet write is timer-raced in this source. Late read success cannot reach that write through the inspected path; `Promise.race` attaches rejection handlers to late read failures. Refusal audit writes are not verified-wallet persistence.
- **Selected block:** [barrier](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L77-L108>) checks both role chain IDs, uses uncached nonnegative bigint heads, selects the lower head, and requires catch-up without selecting the caller's older snapshot. Missing/unequal hashes fail with fixed 503 categories; [post-verification changed hash](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L97-L104>) is refused before persistence. A null block object throws and is safely mapped to unavailable by the barrier/read-stage handler rather than accepted.
- **Proof/history:** [handler options](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/app.ts#L243-L249>) pass the same selected number to rules and proof, and [EIP-1271](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/signing.ts#L62-L64>) forwards it to `readContract`. [Lifetime history](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/verification.ts#L80-L106>) retains absence-at-start, unchanged default start, complete nonce history, and nonce-zero module scans. Receipt minimum is not passed as `fromBlock`. Number pinning/pre/post hashes do not eliminate switching-fallback or ABA reorg limitations; no hash-addressed consensus guarantee is claimed.
- **Minimum/capability:** [canonical parser](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L19-L24>) validates the original top-level field before conversion and RPC; [decoded setup handling](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/app.ts#L217-L219>) does not lose it. [Missing registrationBlock](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/app.ts#L242-L245>) fails closed. The uint64/64-block bounds are implementation limits, not a proof of chain freshness. The production adapter installs a final recheck; arbitrary injected adapters returning a block alone do not establish hash checks, because [recheckRegistrationBlock](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L82-L88>) treats an absent callback as a no-op. This is an adapter trust/fixture limitation, not an observed production bypass.
- **Classification/fallback:** [terminal action wrapper](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L53-L80>) flags typed transport causes only when the overall client action throws. A failed HTTP attempt followed by a successful viem fallback does not reach that catch and therefore does not by itself cause false refusal. Context state is request-local, not adapter-global. Generic swallowed exceptions become a rules/proof refusal rather than an explicitly unknown category; the source does not justify attributing those to rate limits. Provider reasons from the protocol verifier are discarded by the service adapter rather than published.
- **Queue behavior:** [shared URL map](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/chain.ts#L36-L40>) deduplicates public/history queues per adapter. Fetch starts, including retries through these transports, use the same pacing function; [queued abort checks](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/src/registration-reads.ts#L94-L104>) occur before starting and after waits, and rejected requests do not poison the queue. Existing HTTP attempts are bounded; closed registration aborts subsequent queued starts. These are source observations, not measured retry/fallback results.

## UI persistence and owner-material boundary

[Creation](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L294-L303>) awaits confirmed deployment and `bindWallet`, publishes the bound current device/wallet/setup, clears verified/independent-check/review state, then attempts registration. [Storage](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/storage.ts#L29-L36>) resolves writes on transaction completion. [Retry](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L307-L315>) uses the saved wallet rather than redeploying, but may explicitly owner-sign protective cancellation when pending requests exist; the whole retry is not read-only. [Retry visibility](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/App.tsx#L463>) and deposit gating remain present. [The helper](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/src/lifecycle.ts#L18-L26>) snapshots the decimal deployment minimum before its first await and forwards only setup/public identity plus proof. The record keeps the encrypted seed envelope and public receipt block; no owner seed is added to the registration body or report. Ordinary creation/retry success invalidates earlier ownership GETs; the replacement exception is the finding above.

## Evidence still needed

[Service test source](<../../swissquote-wallet/services/swissquote/test/registration-freshness.test.ts>) covers common-head pinning, changed hashes, parser rejection, deadline stages, terminal classification, and queued aborts. [UI component test source](<../../swissquote-wallet/apps/wallet/test/registration-retry.test.tsx#L90-L122>) covers creation refusal/retry, stale GET refusal, and post-logout POST completion. Neither was run here. The latter holds the registration POST, not the challenge before signing, and does not cover replacement registration.

Integration should add/inspect behavioral evidence for lock/logout during challenge and unlock, replacement completion plus late GET/session change, transport-attempt failure followed by fallback success, real retry spacing/shared-role queues, full positive history-start/chunk coverage, and explicit no-change assertions for sender/simulation/gas behavior. Until then, these conclusions remain source-only and as-of-read.
