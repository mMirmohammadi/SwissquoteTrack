## Specification: P0-A–E decisions

### P0-A — Accept protocol enforcement

[Code pins](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L13-L47>) retain main/proxy/admin/token/router/quoter/pool dependencies and add the actual extension hash. Main implementation remains `0xe3e0106227181958abfba960c13d0fe52c733265` with hash `0x210f4eb2374e25850607bdb9f4f41f183c1cc881e932a0e4f304279ef3f6e4f9`. [Configuration checks](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L49-L65>) independently bind extension address, base token, approved scales, supply/borrow rate constants and tracking constants to one explicit base block. Canonical address/word parsing rejects altered or malformed values.

[Production orchestration](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L147-L166>) checks chain identity, obtains the exact base header, pins code/config at that number, binds simulation parent/number/time, and rereads the header to detect a changed hash/time. Every fresh call repeats these checks; no successful pin result is reused across relays. Number-addressed reads plus a stable reread do not prove that a dishonest node or an undetected transient reorg cannot fabricate coherent evidence; that remains the critic's existing RPC trust boundary, not a new patch blocker.

The relied-on pinned extension contains no further delegate/callcode edge. Candidate extension totals/scales are direct storage/pure getter paths. Its other ERC20/permission methods do not become an approved action: [static policy](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/dapps.ts#L68-L114>) still admits only the exact selected supply/withdraw batch.

### P0-B — Accept exact evidence and admitted-domain math

[Named probes](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L74-L86>) preserve all original eight; Compound adds six and uses 31 supply / 29 withdrawal calls. Construction and parsing share the plan. [Allowance checks](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L177-L184>) are deliberately limited to original indices 4–7; tuple probes cannot accidentally enter that loop. Swaps retain their original eight probes and 19 calls, confirmed by the existing regression.

[Tuple parsing](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/compound-math.ts#L21-L37>) enforces exact five/eight-word lengths, int104 sign extension and each unsigned width. Canonical response quantities, complete successful calls, required uint40 time and base+1 number are enforced by [response parsing](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L89-L99>) and [base binding](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L170-L175>). Negative principal is rejected even when displayed borrow rounds to zero.

[Model arithmetic](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/compound-math.ts#L38-L79>) uses old totals/indices to derive utilization and rates, accrues both supply and borrow indices, derives B=floor(P*J/S), Q=floor((B+A)*S/J), C=floor(Q*J/S), and updates total supply by Q-P with borrow principal unchanged. Post-utilization uses both accrued indices. uint256 multiplication, uint64 index/tracking additions, uint104 totals and nonnegative int104/int256 ranges are checked; unsupported debt crossing and non-monotone principal transitions refuse. Immutable baseScale=1e6 makes the candidate reward descale factor exactly 1, so the model's reward division is correct for this approved configuration.

The observed fixed-argument rate probe is correctly a rate-function check, not a post-utilization rate. Every before/after user and totals field is compared to the model; collateral flags/reserved and pause flags are unchanged, while legitimate tracking updates are calculated rather than frozen.

**Exception classification is fail closed.** [Exact evidence wrapper](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L104-L141>) converts generic tuple/math exceptions to typed `DappSimulationError('EFFECTS_MISMATCH', ...)`. [Failover](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L222-L229>) immediately rethrows typed failures, so a malformed mathematical response does not silently become an outage and bypass to another node. Actual transport/client exceptions can fail over; a subsequent node returns green only after its own complete code/config/header/exact evidence passes. Nothing manufactures green effects when every node fails.

### P0-C — Accept positive principal and withdrawals; rewards independently checked

[Admitted transitions](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/compound-math.ts#L67-L79>) retain positive-P supply, partial withdrawal and full withdrawal of B. Empty withdrawal, a>B, repayment and negative starting/ending principal reject. Tiny Q=P supply admits exact zero position credit and no mint rather than fabricating credit.

The positive public report is appropriately limited: its later positive-P actions have **zero elapsed time**, so they prove deployed principal/event behavior but do not prove positive elapsed reward accrual. The independent review additionally checked a source-derived positive elapsed vector, separately from fixture-builder expectations: P=1000000000000, old user tracking index74341550762472, accrued9, old totals from the recorded sample, time1791672132. Elapsed19476 produces trackingSupplyIndex74341688474620, delta137712 and user accrued**137721**. Both supply1000000 and withdrawal1000000 match that result. This is offline source-candidate/model evidence, not a new deployed positive-elapsed replay.

The candidate's `updateBasePrincipal` first accrues with **old principal >=0**, including old P=0, then selects the new tracking index with **new principal >=0**. Therefore a fresh zero-P supply must set user tracking to the accrued **trackingSupplyIndex**, not preserve zero. [Actual model](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/compound-math.ts#L75-L77>) does exactly that, and the saved fresh-supply tuple corroborates it. Tracking subtraction occurs even at P=0; an old tracking index above current supply tracking must revert, not be waved away because multiplication by zero follows. That negative was independently checked. Pause masks1 for supply and4 for withdrawal also reject as the candidate dictates.

**Narrow nonblocking fidelity caveat:** [index calculation](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/compound-math.ts#L59-L66>) evaluates the current supply/borrow rate even when elapsed=0; candidate `accruedInterestIndices` skips rate calculation in that branch. With canonical but pathological totals producing a rate overflow, the model can reject a candidate zero-elapsed action that would otherwise succeed. This is conservative refusal, not unsafe green, and no observed pinned-deployment vector demonstrates the problem. Do not claim unrestricted source-equivalent execution; if supporting that pathological zero-elapsed domain becomes required, guard rate evaluation with elapsed>0 and add an independent regression rather than narrowing withdrawals or ignoring the candidate.

### P0-D — Accept canonical event grammar

[Compound event validation](<../../swissquote-wallet/packages/protocol/src/simulate.ts#L108-L136>) requires all getters to have explicit empty logs, canonical action/approval return bytes, exact per-call event count and order, canonical indexed/data layout and actual amount/recipient. Supply requires Approval(a), then USDC Transfer→Supply→optional mint, then Approval(0); withdrawal requires USDC Transfer→Withdraw→optional burn and no approvals. Missing/extra/duplicate/diverted/reordered logs fail even from allowed contracts. Exact withdrawal USDC transfer amount a is checked.

Mint/burn is floor(abs(Q-P)*J/S), not abs(C-B); the saved positive500000 replay independently distinguishes mint499998 from position delta499999. Tiny Q=P omits mint. Partial withdrawal500000 burns500001; full withdrawal999998 burns999998. All 18 raw stage boundary checks preserve this actual grammar. EURC, debt and unrelated permissions remain unchanged; selected supply permission is zero before/after. This is observational evidence, not a universal storage-diff proof.
